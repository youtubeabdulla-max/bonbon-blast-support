Bonbon Blast — Support

Need help with Bonbon Blast? E-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com and we will answer within 2 working days. It helps if you tell us your device, your iOS version and which level you were on.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The whole game is playable without signing in and without an internet connection. Sign in with Apple is optional and only keeps your progress safe if you change device.

I lost my progress

If you were signed in with Apple and signed into iCloud, reinstall the app and sign in again — your levels and stars come back. Progress made only on a device where you were signed out lives on that device alone.

Are there ads or purchases?

Neither. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no paid boosters. Hammers, shuffles and extra moves are earned by playing and from the daily reward.

Why are there no lives or timers?

By design. You can play as much as you like, whenever you like, and you are never asked to wait or pay to continue.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset Progress, then confirm. This cannot be undone.

The game is in the wrong language

Settings → Language switches between English and Arabic at any time.
