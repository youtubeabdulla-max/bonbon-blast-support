Bonbon Blast — Privacy Policy

Updated 26 October 2026 · applies to Bonbon Blast 1.0.0 and later

Summary

  • Bonbon Blast is free to play. It shows ads, and it sells optional coin packs and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.
  • You never need an account. Sign in with Apple is optional and only syncs your progress.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use the advertising identifier for personalised ads, and we do not sell your personal information.
  • The game asks your age once. Players under 13 only get ads that are suitable for children.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics can be switched off at any time in Settings → Share Usage Data.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your progress: levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, daily-reward and win-streak state.
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, language and the usage-data switch.
  • The age you entered, and whether you bought Remove Ads.
  • If you sign in: the identifier Apple created for this app, and the first name you chose to share.

This stays in the app's private storage. Settings → Reset Progress erases your progress, coins and boosters; deleting the app erases everything.

The age question

When you first open the game it asks how old you are. Your answer stays on your device. The only thing that leaves it is a yes/no signal to our ad partner saying whether ads must be suitable for a child under 13. Your age itself is never sent.

Advertising

Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Ads), a service of Unity Technologies. Ads appear between levels, and you can watch an optional video in the shop to earn free coins. The Remove Ads purchase stops the between-level ads; the free-coin videos stay available because they are always your choice.

To show an ad, measure that it was seen, cap how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, the ad service receives:

  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • your IP address, used to work out your approximate country;
  • an app-specific device identifier (the iOS identifier for vendor, or the Android advertising ID);
  • ad activity: which ads were requested, shown, finished or tapped.

We tell the ad service, for every player, that consent to personalised advertising has not been given and that personal information must not be sold or shared. The app never asks for permission to track you on iOS, so the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it. For players under 13 the ad request is also marked as directed to a child, which limits it to ads that comply with children's privacy laws (COPPA).

Unity's own handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card, bank or billing details. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase went through, which it uses to add your coins or turn ads off. Restore Purchases in the shop asks the store to re-apply Remove Ads on a new device.

Usage statistics

The game uses Unity Analytics to learn which levels are too hard and where players stop. It sends gameplay events — app opened, level started, won or failed, booster used, shop item bought — with a random identifier created for this install, plus basic device information. It does not include your name, email or Apple ID. Switch it off in Settings → Share Usage Data; doing so stops collection and asks Unity to delete what was already gathered.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Apple gives the app a stable user identifier made for this app only, and — on the first sign-in and only if you allow it — your name and email address (Apple lets you share a private relay address instead). We use the identifier only to keep your progress with you. You can sign out or delete the account in Settings; deleting it unlinks your Apple ID and erases the iCloud copy of your progress.

iCloud

If you are signed into iCloud, your progress is also kept in your own private iCloud key-value storage so it can follow you to your other devices. It lives in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot read it.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information for money.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No chat, no user-generated content, and no way for players to contact each other.

Children

Bonbon Blast is made for all ages. Because children may play, the game asks every player's age and treats anyone under 13 as a child for advertising, as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents who believe their child has given us information can e-mail us and we will delete it. Parents can stop purchases with the parental controls on the device (Screen Time on iPhone, purchase authentication on Google Play).

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Because almost everything stays on your device, deleting the app removes it; for anything else, e-mail us and we will reply within 30 days. Residents of the EU, UK and California have these rights in law, and may also complain to their local data protection authority.
