Bonbon Blast — Privacy Policy

Updated 26 September 2026 · applies to Bonbon Blast 1.0.0 and later

Summary

Bonbon Blast has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK and no game server. You can play the entire game without an account and without an internet connection. We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The only information the app can receive about you is what Apple gives it if you choose the optional Sign in with Apple, and that is used for one purpose: keeping your level progress when you move to another device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars, best scores, boosters, daily-reward and win-streak state.
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration and language.
  • If you sign in: the identifier Apple created for this app, and the first name you chose to share.

This lives in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset Progress.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Signing in is completely optional; the full game works signed out. If you use it, Apple provides the app with:

  • a stable user identifier that Apple creates specifically for this app and no other;
  • your name and email address, only on the very first sign-in and only if you allow it. Apple lets you hide your real address and share a private relay address instead.

We use the identifier only to attach your saved progress to you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build a profile from it, and it is never sold or shared. Sign out at any time in Settings, which deletes it from your device, or revoke the app entirely in iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

iCloud

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, your progress is also stored in your own private iCloud key-value storage so it can follow you to your other devices. That data lives in your iCloud account, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot read it. Turning off iCloud for Bonbon Blast in your device settings stops this; your local progress is unaffected.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment information.
  • No analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and contains nothing that identifies a player. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity's runtime may collect limited anonymous device information as described in Unity's own privacy policy; optional Unity data collection is disabled where the engine allows it.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes the Apple identifier from your device. Deleting the app removes everything else, and you can clear iCloud copies from your device's iCloud settings. If you would like anything else removed, e-mail us and we will act within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds online features — we will update this page and note it in the app's store listing.
